---
name: Circuit history compatibility
description: Preserve existing lift analytics and logs when extending exercise programming
---

Circuit support must remain compatible with existing per-exercise, per-set history. Treat one round as one set of each circuit exercise; rest is between rounds. Muscle-group categories are accessory classifications, not new competition lifts.

**Why:** The user requested circuits and muscle categories on an established tracker whose recorded workouts, volume calculations, and competition-lift max trends must remain intact.

**How to apply:** Extend prescriptions without migrating or rewriting historical results. Do not count circuit rounds twice in volume or let an accessory category become eligible for competition-lift max trends.
