---
name: Lifter and group membership rules
description: Unified lifters, additive leadership, membership lifecycle and history preservation
---

All non-admin users are lifters. A lifter can create one named group and become
its group leader. A lifter may belong to only one group at a time, including
one they lead. Every lifter retains workspace library access and can create
and run their own programs even while grouped.

**Why:** The user replaced fixed coach/client roles and explicitly confirmed
that joining a group must not restrict independent programming.

**How to apply:** Treat leadership as current membership authority. Joining,
leaving and deletion must not move accounts between team/personal workspaces,
change stable user IDs, or delete blocks, logs, measurements or comments.
Only a group leader may delete a group; members can leave. Current leaders
review members and assign programs. Former leaders lose that access.

The user requested bhatta lead **smurf squad**, with Danny and Derek as members
and their deload history retained. Other existing coach relationships are
converted without rewriting training records. Admins remain read-only across
all lifters/groups in their own workspace and can invite independent lifters.

**Why:** The user explicitly required continuity for current training and
continued separation of team and personal data.

**How to apply:** Preserve uploader ownership and assignment snapshots. Keep
old discussion content intact, changing only visible role labels. Do not
re-run legacy relationship conversion after groups have been left or deleted,
or roll back to old authorization code that ignores group lifecycle changes.
